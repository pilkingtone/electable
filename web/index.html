How Electable works

  1. National environment. A weighted generic-ballot polling average, blended with a fundamentals prior: the 2024 House vote, shifted by the historical midterm penalty against the president's party and by presidential approval.
  2. Race prior. Each state's or district's 2024 presidential lean relative to the nation, plus the national environment, incumbency and (for federal races) FEC fundraising.
  3. Race polls. Recency-, sample-, and pollster-quality-weighted average, adjusted for national movement since each poll; partisan sponsor polls are down-weighted.
  4. Blend. Prior and polls are combined by inverse variance, using error sizes estimated from 1998–2022 polls vs. results.
  5. Simulate. 20,000 correlated elections with fat-tailed national, polling, regional, and race-level errors give win probabilities, seat totals, and tipping points.

Margins are Democratic minus Republican, two-party, in points. Independents running in place of a Democrat are shown in the Democratic column; they count toward Democratic control only if marked as caucusing with Democrats.
